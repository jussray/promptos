    promptos / anatomy

    System Prompt Anatomy

    The 7-layer structure behind every prompt in this library. Order matters.

    Role
    Set the exact identity of the agent. Example: founder-grade market research analyst, or senior TypeScript repo mechanic.
    Objective
    One clear mission. Don't combine research, planning, implementation, and review in a single vague ask.
    Context
    Feed product, repo, stage, audience, stack, and constraints so the model reasons from your reality instead of defaults.
    Constraints
    Forbid guessing, fabricated sources, placeholder fixes, broad rewrites, or deletion of working functionality.
    Instructions
    Make steps ordered: assess, rank, patch, validate. Claude especially benefits from tagged XML-like structure.
    Output format
    Specify the exact shape you want back: diagnosis, checklist, patch, risks — or segment map, white space, confidence.
    Evidence
    Paste the logs, URLs, data, screenshots, schema, or code that should anchor the answer.
