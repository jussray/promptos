## Why this exists (the diagnosis)

Instagram's top three ranking signals (Mosseri, Jan 2025) are **watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach**. Comments are not top-3. A post built only to be watched passively earns none of them. The business accounts' record proves it: views up to 3.7× baseline, saves/shares/comments at zero. Every post under this system is built to earn one named signal on purpose.

## The laws (implant into every social pipeline)

1. **HOOK IN FRAME 1.** The hook is spoken AND shown as big on-screen text in the first frame. Proposition lands by second 3. Open mid-action — no logo, no hello, no intro. (TikTok: 90% of ad recall impact lands in the first 6 seconds; Metricool 2025: average watch time is 3.75s.)
2. **ONE JOB PER POST.** Every post is assigned exactly one job before it is built — SAVE, SEND, or COMMENT — and carries exactly one CTA matched to that job. Multiple competing asks kill all of them.
   - **SAVE job** = reference value: checklist, steps, framework, exact numbers. On-screen checklist in the Reel + a screenshot-able end frame held 2 seconds + one specific ask ("Save this for your next wash day / signing / launch"). A carousel companion (the save engine) follows the next day.
   - **SEND job** = a specific person needs this: relatable identity moment, a tip for a friend, a defensible niche hot take, one quotable line that survives forwarding. Never "tag 3 friends" — design so they *send* it.
   - **COMMENT job** = ask for the *content* of a reply: a specific experience question, a two-option debate, fill-in-the-blank, an invited unpopular opinion, "comment your X — I'll test it next." Never "comment YES," never engagement-for-access.
3. **EDUTAINMENT IS THE HOUSE FORMAT.** 66% of social users call edutainment the most engaging brand content (Sprout) — one useful lesson delivered with tension, character, and a payoff. This is the entertainment remix of the standing explainer-demo standard, not a replacement for it: the app process still shows on screen, in the app's own words.
4. **MYTH-BUST IS A STANDING FORMAT.** Negative hook ("Stop believing X") → proof on screen → saveable checklist end-frame. All three lanes have expensive myths; the correction is the shareable unit.
5. **SERIES RUN IN SEASONS.** Recurring franchises with the same premise and opening line, committed 6–8 episodes before judging. Existing: Truth Weaver Law of the Day. The joke punches at the problem/industry, never the customer; the product is the resolution, never the joke; visual identity stays consistent even when the format flexes.
6. **LENGTH LAW.** Reels 35–55 seconds (Instagram engagement peaks 45–60s, collapses past 180s; Reels over 3 minutes are not recommended to new audiences). Never stretch a thin idea.
7. **ORIGINAL + CLEAN, ALWAYS.** Original content only (Meta replaces copies in recommendations; 10+ unoriginal posts in 30 days removes an account from recommendations). Clean native export per platform — no cross-platform watermarks, no borders, no majority-text frames, no low-res.
8. **NEVER LIST.** Engagement bait ("like if…", "comment YES", "tag 3 friends", "follow for Part 2/win/unlock"); clickbait with no payoff; polished studio promos as organic; late literal trend copies with no niche twist; generic day-in-the-life with no specific frame; exaggerated health/legal/financial outcome claims (recommendation-ineligible AND against Truth Weaver's education-not-advice law and JBH's real-or-nothing law).

## Hook formulas (pick one per post; hook text in frame 1)

- **Curiosity gap:** "The [result] nobody shows you — wait for step 3." (withhold the payoff to the end)
- **Bold claim:** "[Specific claim] in [timeframe] — and I'll prove it."
- **Negative:** "Stop doing X. Do Y instead."
- **Question-first:** a specific, answerable question the audience has actually asked themselves.
- **Number-led:** "[3/5] [mistakes/signs] — #1 is the worst."
- **Pattern interrupt:** result first / mid-action open, context second.
- **Relatable POV:** "POV: you [specific frustrating moment everyone in the niche knows]."

## Measurement (replaces raw views as the headline)

Per Reel, in this order: average watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach, saves per reach, % non-follower reach. Kill-fast still applies: a format that earns no signal after a fair trial (one season / 5+ posts) is cut, not optimized forever.
