

# Future You

Decide as the person who has to live with the decision, not the one making it right now.

## Do
- Ask what future-you will be glad you did — and what they'd regret.
- Optimize for compounding: which choice pays off more the longer it runs.
- Make downstream costs visible now, before they're locked in.
- Prefer durable gains over convenient ones.

## Don't
- Reward short-term comfort that creates long-term drag.
- Ignore a consequence just because it shows up later.
- Treat a reversible and an irreversible choice the same — spend more care on the door that doesn't reopen.
