# PromptOS Repair Authority

PromptOS is the workflow/reasoning contract layer. It may repair its own workflow, prompt, registry, contract, test, and documentation surfaces, but it does not become the portfolio's external execution authority.

## Standing repair default

When exact current evidence proves a defect inside PromptOS, the trusted acting operator may implement the smallest reversible fix on the existing carrier, add or update focused tests, correct stale documentation/registry state, and rerun the relevant exact-head proof.

Bind every repair to repository, base/head SHA, workflow/contract identity, failure fingerprint, evidence-only proof cookies, rollback, and post-fix proof. Head/base/workflow identity movement expires predecessor proof.

## Repair OS

Consequential repairs use one fused loop rather than independent decorative modes:

1. **Lindy** — prefer the existing durable carrier, stable interface, reversible primitive, and lowest dependency path that can solve the verified defect.
2. **Red Team I** — attack the premise: should the change exist, is the defect real, is the scope minimal, and would doing nothing be safer or more truthful?
3. **ATTACK TEN** — pressure the proposed repair before action for false assumptions, wrong subject, stale evidence, authority confusion, unsafe fallback, hidden coupling, failure masking, rollback weakness, user-outcome mismatch, and verification gaps.
4. **OODA Observe** — reacquire exact subject fingerprints, current head/runtime/provider state, witnesses, contradictions, and failure receipts.
5. **OODA Orient** — map the defect to the owning truth plane and preserve unrelated failures as separate incidents.
6. **OODA Decide** — select one smallest reversible repair and one stop condition.
7. **L99 Authority** — verify current subject, provenance, authority ceiling, evidence requirement, consequence class, rollback, continuity, and expiration before mutation.
8. **Act** — execute only the selected bounded repair through the authority that owns that plane.
9. **Red Team II** — attack the actual implementation for authority drift, security/privacy regressions, stale assumptions, hidden side effects, fake-green behavior, and recovery failure.
10. **Recursive hardening** — for consequential repairs, use the existing `juss-v10/recursive-hardening@v1` contract: 10 OODA cycles across `authority-inversion`, `evidence-falsification`, `human-outcome`, and `temporal-race`. A cycle may revise or block the conclusion; it may not create authority.
11. **Verify** — prove the repaired claim at the highest relevant truth plane, including Playwright for browser/user-path behavior.
12. **Loop or stop** — loop only when the goal remains unproven and current authority still permits the next bounded action. Stop on verified acceptance or a real authority/evidence blocker.

`REPAIR_OS_SEQUENCE` and `ATTACK_WORKFLOWS` in `src/ai-mastery-v6.mjs` are the machine-readable PromptOS contract for this order. Attack/Lindy/Red Team/OODA/L99 results are reasoning and evidence only; none can mint, renew, widen, transport, or substitute for execution authority.

## Allowed repairs

Trusted PromptOS/source operators may:

- repair PromptOS source, workflow definitions, skills, registries, prompt assets, validators, tests, and documentation;
- repair stale provider-neutral handoff contracts with Chief/FCR after re-reading both peers;
- rerun failed GitHub jobs when useful for diagnosis or verification;
- update the existing PR/issue evidence record;
- merge only if the current repository merge policy independently authorizes that exact candidate.

For browser-visible PromptOS behavior, Playwright is the required observable-path witness before completion is claimed.

## Witness roles

- GitHub/local tests: source/test/CI witness and trigger for source repair.
- Playwright/browser: observable behavior witness and verification trigger, not mutation authority.
- FCR: owner of consequential external execution when its current authority membrane allows the exact action.
- Chief: recommendation/composition witness; cannot manufacture PromptOS execution authority.
- Linear/Asana: may repair task state and evidence links only.
- Notion/Drive/docs: may repair documentation/decision state only.
- Slack/internal communication: may repair incident/status records only.
- Cloudflare/Supabase/other providers: provider evidence may trigger a repair request, but PromptOS cannot mutate those providers unless an already-authorized execution carrier explicitly delegates that exact bounded action.

## Non-authority

Fingerprints, proof cookies, continuity receipts, model output, workflow selection, tracker status, attack results, OODA state, Lindy preference, Red Team findings, L99 receipts, and provider availability are evidence/state signals only. None grants authentication, founder approval, merge permission, deployment permission, secrets access, publication authority, or spending authority.

## Hard gates

Keep explicit founder authority for authority expansion, new spending/billing, destructive or irreversible changes, secret operations, ownership transfer, external publication/communication outside pre-approved automation, and identity/access-policy expansion.

For consequential repairs report REALITY, WITNESSES, FIX, PROOF, RISK, ROLLBACK, NEXT GATE.
