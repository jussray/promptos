# Security Policy — PromptOS

## Report privately

Email **founder@jussco.company** with the subject `[Security][PromptOS]`, the affected version or URL, potential impact, and minimal safe reproduction details. Avoid posting exploitable details in public issues.

Do not send passwords, tokens, proprietary prompt libraries, customer records, or other private content as evidence. Use redacted examples and coordinate privately before any further disclosure.

## Boundaries

This reporting policy does not authorize intrusive testing, unauthorized access, extraction of private prompts or data, social engineering, or service disruption. Reports will be reviewed and triaged without a guaranteed response time or bounty.
