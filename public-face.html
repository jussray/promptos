PromptOS · Public product definition

Intent in. Governed workflow out.

PromptOS turns natural-language founder goals into reusable prompts, workflows, skills, agents, and execution requests without silently changing the founder's intent or authority.

Direct answer

PromptOS is an intention compiler: it structures what the founder wants into durable operating assets with context, constraints, authority, verification requirements, rollback, and stop conditions.

What it does

  • Compiles intent into structured prompts and workflows.
  • Preserves approved instructions as reusable operating assets.
  • Keeps provider-specific syntax separate from the founder's underlying intent.
  • Separates advisory output from execution, deployment, and publication authority.

Authority boundary

A prompt, workflow, skill, plugin declaration, or generated mission does not authorize execution by itself. PromptOS may prepare a bounded execution request; Chief AI and Founder Control Room remain separate governed layers with their own evidence and authority requirements.

Public surface truth

PromptOS has a separately governed GitHub Pages publication capability. ChatGPT Sites is a different provider surface, and this repository currently records the PromptOS ChatGPT Site identity as unverified.

No ChatGPT Site hostname, custom domain, publication state, or provider equivalence is inferred here.
